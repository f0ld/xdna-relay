# Baseline 2026-11-24 — FLM (NPU) vs llama.cpp Vulkan (iGPU), Qwen3.5-4B

Machine: Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, 64 GB, kernel 7.3.4, platform profile `low-power`.
NPU: FLM v1.0.9 `qwen3.5:4b` (Q4NX), `--pmode powersaver` (performance differed <2%).
iGPU: llama.cpp `6b0f75b` build-vulkan, `Qwen3.5-4B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf`, `-ngl 99 -fa on`.
Tool: `bench/probe_openai.py` (server-side counters), greedy, filler prompt.
