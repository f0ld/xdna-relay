# T-021 — llama.cpp speculative decoding on the iGPU (Qwen3.5-4B target)

Platform profile `performance`, on battery. Target `Qwen3.5-4B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf`, draft
`Qwen3.5-0.8B-Q8_0.gguf`, both `-ngl 99 -fa on`, greedy, 256 tokens, thinking off.
Tool: `bench/spec_probe.py` (4 prompts). Baseline without speculation ≈ 20.8 tok/s.

Output check (greedy): code and rewrite identical to no-spec for stock and patched; explain and
story diverge mid-text for **both** stock and patched (coherent alternative wording) →
batched-vs-single-token float near-ties, not the patch.

Per-cycle accounting (story, n=3): ~3 draft steps × 16 ms + draft re-processing the verify
batch ~17 ms + target verify ~54 ms ≈ 120–130 ms per cycle for ~2.1 tokens. The draft costs
more than the verify — same-device speculation with a 0.8B draft cannot win much on a 4B
target. Moving the draft off the iGPU (NPU, concurrent) is exactly the T-025 hypothesis.
